Dataset: PulseDB / VitalDB
===========================

Source (download instructions, license, full dataset):
https://github.com/pulselabteam/PulseDB

Partitions used in this project (PulseDB VitalDB supplementary subsets):
- VitalDB_CalFree_Test_Subset
- VitalDB_Train_Subset

These correspond to the .h5 files loaded by Scripts/bp_lgbm/data.py's
load_PulseDB_sup_ds() (e.g. Features_VitalDB_Train_Subset.h5,
Features_VitalDB_CalFree_Test_Subset.h5, and the Clean_Features_*.h5
outputs produced from them by Scripts/fiducial_quality_filtering/).
Place the downloaded .h5 files in the folder pointed to by
PULSE_DB_SUP_DIR in Scripts/bp_lgbm/local_paths.py (see
local_paths.py.example).
